The third rename in two years, and the first one nobody did by hand.

## The problem

A prop rename across 400 call sites is a week of mechanical edits and a review
nobody can read. It also cannot be verified: a missed site is a runtime warning
in a branch someone opens next month.

## The approach

A codemod over the TypeScript AST rather than a regex over text, so a prop
spread and a re-export are cases rather than casualties. It emits a report of
every file it touched and every one it skipped with a reason.

## The outcome

One commit, and the skipped list was short enough to read. The report is the
artifact that made the diff reviewable.

A hospitality booking product where availability lived in four places and agreed
in none of them. The calendar was the product, and it was the least trustworthy
screen in it.

## The problem

Availability was derived at render time in three separate components, each with
its own idea of what a blocked day meant. Support fielded double-bookings weekly.

## The approach

One availability model on the server, one query per view, and a calendar that
reads it rather than recomputing it. The write path grew a constraint so an
overlapping hold fails in the database instead of in a review.

## The outcome

Double-bookings stopped being a support category. The team shipped a second
property type in a week, because availability was one thing to extend rather
than four.

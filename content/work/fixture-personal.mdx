Measuring a room with a tape and a notes app produces a number and no picture.
This produces the picture.

## The problem

Furniture shopping asks a spatial question in a spreadsheet's language: three
dimensions, a doorway and a radiator, held in your head at once.

## The approach

A room is a box you type dimensions into, furniture snaps to walls and to other
furniture, and the whole scene is a URL. No accounts, no persistence layer, no
backend at all.

## The outcome

The desk did not fit. Knowing that before delivery day was the entire point.
